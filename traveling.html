TRAVELING

I love going places and exploring new cultures and sights! Below are some of the places I've been to and their reviews!

Hawaii, United States

Hawaii is definitely my favorite place I've visited so far; I went to O'ahu and the Big Island. There is so much to do, with activities for every type of person. There are many hikes, beaches, farmer's markets, gift shops, restaurants...and even more! The view on both islands was breathtaking - the mountains, volcano (on the Big Island!), and stargazing! If you are looking for an adventurous trip or just a getaway, I think Hawaii is perfect. Here are some awesome places you can visit on O'ahu:

  • Dole Plantation
  • Diamond Head
  • Pearl Harbor
  • Waikiki Beach
  • Kailua Beach

Paris, France

Paris feels like I can go there a million times! It is full of rich culture...and amazing food! There are many shops, bakeries, architecture to take pictures of, etc. But wait! If you are an art lover like more - or even if you aren't - there is so much art to appreciate! If you are ready to experience all the things Paris has to offer, below are some places that are a must!:

  • Louvre
  • Eiffel Tower
  • Notre Dame Cathedral
  • Arc de Triomphe
  • Montmartre/Sacre Coeur
  • Luxembourg Gardens

Disney World, United States

Disney is the ultimate place for fun and to feel like a kid! There are so many rides for everyone, and I know that because I do NOT like rollercoasters! There were plenty of rides I could go on! The Magic Kingdom is truly a magical time with princess visits and classic character rides. The Animal Kindgom boasts many different animals with real-live animal shows!
