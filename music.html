MUSIC

I really love music! I play two instruments and I am trying to learn guitar. I also enjoy listening to my Spotify playlists at home. At school, I participate in our band, choir, select choir, and select jazz band.

I have gotten into music since 5th grade when we had the option to play an instrument. I knew absolutely nothing about music, and it seemed like something fun to try. I chose clarinet, and my teacher taught me how to play and read music. Since then, I am still playing clarinet!

In 8th grade, I decided I wanted to try to play tenor saxophone. I was given the instrument and a practice book, and I learned the basics on my own because it was so similar to clarinet. Later, I played in my school jazz band.

There are various music artsist and songs that I love, so check out my Spotify playlists below! I really like Taylor Swift, Demi Lovato, and Olivia Rodrigo, but there are plenty of other artists I also listen to.
