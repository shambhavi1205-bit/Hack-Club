This project is a personal website about me, featuring some of my hobbies and information about myself. Also, fun stuff!

I made this website using HTML, CSS, and a tiny bit of JavaScript.

#Features
- Cool topographic background on the entire website, except for modals
- Auto typing text
- Flowing gradient background on website header (my name)
- Sticky navbar
- Navbar blurs the background behind it
- Custom scroll bar on the entire website, including modals
- DARK MODE
  - Different topographic background color
  - Different colors
  - The toggle changes with each click
- Music player modal
  - Opens up a modal
  - Plays a song
  - User can exit out of the modal by clicking the "x" button or clicking on the window outside of the modal
- Navbar uses anchor links to scroll smoothly to sections
- Informative glass cards that get slightly larger when the user hovers over them
- The background behind the glass cards is blurred
- 4 interest modals
  - Music modal: what instruments I play, my SPOTIFY playlists
  - Traveling modal: travel recommendations with reviews
  - Art modal: art gallery with a informative overlay on top of the art images that show up when the user hovers overs an image
  - Books modal: book recommendations with reviews
- Projects section
  - Several glass cards that get larger when hovered over
  - Two links fade in when the user hovers over the card, which open up in a new tab
- Achievements section
  - Sliding marquee
  - When user hovers over the marquee, it stops, allowing the user to read the text
- Contact section
  - Two image links that bring the user to my email and github page in a separate tab
- On the modals, as the user starts to scroll down, a scroll back to top button pops up, bringing the user back to the top of the modal so they can click the "x" button

#How to Use
- Using this website is pretty straightforward. But here are some features that might require some clicking.
- The music player at the bottom right corner of your screen can be clicked, opening up a modal, where you have to scroll down a little bit to actually play the music
- The four modals in the Interests section can be opened by just clicking anywhere on the cards
   - Scroll down in the Music modal to listen to my spotify playlists
   - Hover over the images in the Art modal to learn more about them (an overlay will fade in)
- Hover over the glass cards in the Projects section to go to the links provided
- Hover over the marquee in the Achievements section so you can get time to read it without the cards moving and have it be confusing:-)
- Click on the gmail and github logo to go to my profiles or contact me in the Contacts section
